Items and dimensions

Set each item once; every record then computes its quantity from the From and To chainages. Lengths (m) need nothing; areas (m²) a width; volumes (m³) a width and a height, depth or thickness; litres a width and spray rate. Number, tonne and sum items take the quantity on each record. Bills from the Road BOQ Estimator arrive with these filled in.

Send to the office

Export the period's records as one file and send it to the office (e-mail, WhatsApp or USB). The office imports it into the Progress and Valuation app; the Engineer approves there.
